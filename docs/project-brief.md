## Purpose

Establish company credibility for government and institutional procurement. The audience is
procurement officers and institutional buyers checking that the company is real, reachable
and legitimate — not retail shoppers.

## Positioning

A supplier and trading company providing several categories of goods and selected services:

- Computers and information technology
- Office supplies and equipment
- Electronics and technical supplies
- General institutional equipment
- Selected supporting goods and services

Procurement presence: **SIPLah Toko Ladang** (client-provided store link). **Mbizmarket** is
pending verification of the correct company profile and stays hidden until verified.

Tax status: **Pengusaha Kena Pajak (PKP)**, confirmed by the client for public use.

## Out of scope

Prices, stock quantities, shopping carts, checkout, contact forms, any backend, user accounts,
English content, paid services.

## Design direction — Modern Institutional Elegance

Premium, modern and credible: exceptional editorial typography, distinctive composition,
refined spacing and hierarchy, restrained purposeful motion, strong corporate identity,
excellent mobile experience. Avoid generic SaaS/AI-template aesthetics, heavy gradients,
glassmorphism, repetitive card grids and decorative animation.

Palette and rules: [design-standards.md](design-standards.md).

## Open inputs from the client

Tracked live by `npm run content:check`. At least one contact channel (WhatsApp or email) is
required before launch; the rest are optional and omitted until confirmed. At the time of writing:

- Official WhatsApp number and email address
- Confirmation of office hours (Google Maps shows Mon–Fri 09.00–17.00, Sat 09.00–15.00)
- Verified Mbizmarket profile URL
- Vector logo source (SVG/AI/PDF), if one exists
